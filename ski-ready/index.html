Your five sessions

One to one, with me. In Richmond (GreenSpace or Zenith gym) or online if work keeps you moving.

  1. 1

    Ski check

    Where your legs, knees, hips and balance are now, and what's most likely to let you down on day three. You leave with your starting numbers.

  2. 2

    Legs that last

    Leg endurance for long runs: holds, slow squats and lunges, so your thighs aren't burning by lunchtime.

  3. 3

    Knees and landings

    Side-to-side strength, single-leg control and soft landings. This is the knee work most people skip.

  4. 4

    Back and trunk

    A trunk that holds you steady when the snow doesn't, and a back that still works after a week of chairlifts.

  5. 5

    Re-test and holiday plan

    We re-run your ski check so you see the change. Then I hand you a short warm-up for the slope and a reset for the evening.

Plus: a simple plan to keep going between sessions, and me on WhatsApp if something niggles.

Is it for you?

Good fit

  • You're 40 to 65 and you've got a ski trip booked
  • You want to ski the whole week, not just the first two days
  • You've had a dodgy knee or back before and don't want a repeat
  • You're skiing with the kids and want to keep up

Not for you if

  • You're recovering from an injury right now. See your physio or GP first, then come back to me
  • You want a ski technique lesson. I get your body ready; the instructor does the rest

Questions

I'm not very fit. Is that a problem?

No. Session one finds your starting point and we build from there. Most of the people I coach are 40 to 65 and haven't trained properly in years.

Can I do it online?

Yes. All you need is a bit of floor space. If you travel for work, we can do sessions from your hotel room.

My trip is in less than five weeks.

Book the call anyway. We can fit sessions closer together, and even two or three sessions plus the holiday plan will help.

What if I've hurt my knee before?

Tell me on the call. If it's still sore or you're mid-rehab, check with your physio or GP first. If you've been cleared, we'll build around it.

Not medical advice. If you have a heart, joint or balance condition, get your GP's OK before starting something new.

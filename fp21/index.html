Sound familiar?

You used to be fit. Now the noise never stops.

How it works

Three weeks, one simple routine.

Week 1

Breathe and reset

Slow the system down. Simple breathwork to switch off the noise, plus gentle mobility for hips, back and shoulders.

Week 2

Move and strengthen

Build on it. Bodyweight strength and balance work that keeps you capable for the next 20 years.

Week 3

Lock it in

Turn it into a habit that sticks after the 21 days, so you're not back to square one in December.

Questions

Do I need a gym or kit?

No. Everything is done at home in about 15 minutes. A bit of floor space is all you need.

I'm not very fit right now. Is it for me?

Yes. That's exactly who it's for. Every session has an easier option, and it starts gently.

What if I miss a day?

Pick it back up the next day. The live calls are recorded, and nobody's keeping score but you.

I've got an injury or a health condition.

Have a word with your GP before you start. Tell me about it when you join and I'll help you adapt the moves.

What happens after I join?

You'll get a welcome page straight away with the next steps, and I'll add you to the group before we start on 2 November.

Is this the same as HeNix?

No. HeNix is the in-person men's circle in Twickenham. Future-Proof 21 is online, so you can join from anywhere. You're welcome at both.

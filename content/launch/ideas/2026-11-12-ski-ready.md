## The idea in one line

Same offer you already have (Sharper Start, 5 × 1:1 for £375), sold to one clear group: people aged 40–65 with a ski trip booked for Christmas or February half-term. Nothing new to build.

## Why now

- Nuffield Health says knee injuries are around a third of all skiing injuries, and to start getting ready **at least 6 to 8 weeks before the trip**. ([Nuffield Health](https://www.nuffieldhealth.com/article/skiing-knee-injuries-prevention-treatment))
- Richmond schools break up for Christmas around **18 Dec 2026**, and February half-term is around **12–22 Feb 2027**. ([Richmond Council term times 2026/27](https://www.richmond.gov.uk/media/ig1f3s5g/richmond-school-streets-term-times-for-2627.pdf))
- So: Christmas skiers need to start **this week or next**. Half-term skiers need to start **by mid-December**. Two waves of asks from one idea.
- Fits your LinkedIn line too: "Coaching that travels with you".

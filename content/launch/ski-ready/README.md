## 1. The 5 sessions (your coaching plan; change anything you like)

**Session 1: Ski check (baseline).** Chat: trip dates, ski level, old injuries. Then test and write down:
- Wall sit hold (seconds)
- Side hop and stick: 5 each side, how steady is the landing (knee caving in? wobble?)
- Single-leg squat to a bench: reps each side, and the knee path
- Knee-to-wall ankle test (cm)
- 30-second single-leg stand, eyes open, each side

Finish with their between-sessions plan: wall sit, hinge and a 10-minute walk-up-hills habit, 3 times a week.

**Session 2: Legs that last.** Wall sit holds, tempo goblet squats (3 seconds down), split squats, step-ups. Finish with "ski intervals": 40 seconds of squat pulses or a low hold, 20 seconds off, × 6. That's the lunchtime-thigh-burn session.

**Session 3: Knees and landings.** Side hops and stick, skater bounds (start small), lateral lunges, single-leg RDL, side-lying adductor work, hamstring curls (slider or band). Coach the knee tracking over the toes on every landing.

**Session 4: Back and trunk.** Pallof press, dead bugs, side planks, suitcase carries, plus a hip and upper-back mobility flow for after long chairlift days. Add a "getting up from the snow" drill: down to the floor and back up with poles or without, both sides.

**Session 5: Re-test and holiday plan.** Re-run the Session 1 tests and show them the numbers side by side (send them a photo of it, it's a great testimonial moment). Hand over:
- **Morning on the slope (8 min):** leg swings, walking lunges with a twist, 10 squats, 5 side hops each side, ankle rocks.
- **Evening reset (10 min):** calf and quad stretch, hip flexor stretch, 90/90 hips, lying twist, legs up the wall.

**After the trip:** WhatsApp them the day they're back (script below). Ask for a review and a referral.

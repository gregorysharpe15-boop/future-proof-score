Wall sit

Back flat on a wall, slide down until your thighs are as close to level as is comfy.
Hold 20–30 seconds. Build to 60.
3 rounds, 2–3 times a week.
Why: thighs that last the whole run, not just the first one.
4/7
2

Side hop and stick

Hop sideways off one leg and land soft on the other.
Knee over your toes, not caving in. Hold the landing for 2 seconds.
6 each side. Start small, go further as it feels solid.
Why: skiing is side to side. Your knees need to be ready for it.
5/7
3

One-leg hinge

Stand on one leg, soft knee. Tip forward from the hips, reach low, stand back up tall.
Hold a wall or chair at first if you need to.
8 each side, 2–3 rounds.
Why: balance, plus strong hamstrings that back up your knee.
